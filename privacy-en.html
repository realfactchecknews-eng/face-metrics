Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

This Policy describes how the FaceRate web service (facerate.ru) and its Telegram bots (the "Service") process personal data under Russian Federal Law No. 152-FZ "On Personal Data". Data controller: the owner of FaceRate. Contact: @faceratemanager, @FaceRateSupport_bot, Partners@FaceRate.online. The Russian version is the governing one.

A photo of a face is treated as biometric personal data. It is processed only with your consent, given by uploading the photo and accepting the Terms. You may withdraw consent at any time by stopping using the Service.

1. What we process

  • Uploaded photo — used for one request and not stored on our servers.
  • Analysis results — scores and report text, kept for up to 60 days and looked up by an irreversible hash of the photo; the photo itself is not stored.
  • Coaching data — dates, scores and texts of progress measurements, personal tips, current plan week.
  • Telegram account data — ID, name, username and profile photo link. We never ask for your phone number.
  • Payment data — plan, amount, method, time, order number, promo code used. Card and wallet details are processed only by the payment service.
  • Promotions — giveaway entries, promo code redemptions, referrals.
  • TON wallet address — only if you link a wallet, to check the FACE token balance.
  • Support messages and feedback form answers.
  • Technical data — IP address (rate limiting and abuse protection) and anonymous step counters on the site.
  • Yandex.Metrica data — pages visited, referrer, device and browser type, IP address, cookie identifiers.

2. Third parties

  • OpenRouter (USA) and AI models by Google (USA), with xAI (USA) as a fallback — photo analysis and report generation;
  • Telegram — login, notifications, bots, Telegram Stars payments;
  • Lava.top — card and SBP payments; CryptoBot — crypto payments;
  • Cloudflare (USA) — proxy, protection and account data storage; GitHub (USA) — static site hosting;
  • Yandex LLC (Russia) — site statistics (Yandex.Metrica);
  • Google Fonts (USA) — web fonts; your browser sends your IP address;
  • tonapi.io, toncenter.com — FACE balance check for a linked wallet;
  • giveaway partners — winner contact details, with consent, only to deliver a prize.

Some of them process data outside Russia. We never sell your data.

3. Giveaways and messages

By entering a giveaway you agree that the results, with your Telegram name or username and account ID, are published in the Service's channel. The Bots send service messages, reminders, coaching tasks and offers; you can stop reminders and offers with the "Не писать мне" button or via support.

4. Retention

  • Uploaded photo — not kept after the request.
  • Analysis results — up to 60 days. Login session — up to 30 days.
  • Account, coaching and payment data — while the account is used and afterwards as long as required for accounting, disputes and legal obligations.
  • Local browser data — until you delete it; the temporary photo copy — up to 30 minutes.

5. Your rights

You may withdraw consent, request information about your data, and ask for it to be corrected, blocked or deleted via @FaceRateSupport_bot or Partners@FaceRate.online. Payment records may be kept longer where the law requires it. You can delete local data by clearing site data in your browser.

6. Cookies and local storage

  • Yandex.Metrica cookies for visit statistics. You can disable them in your browser; the Service keeps working.
  • Browser localStorage — Telegram login, your last result and score history, acceptance of the Terms, language, report tone and other settings, and the temporary photo copy during login. Kept only on your device.
  • No advertising cookies or ad tracking.

7. Changes

We may update this Policy. A new version takes effect when published on this page.
